Our Story

COMP3612 Assign 1 began with a simple belief: every programmer deserves clothes that look exceptional and feel extraordinary. Inspired by the heroes of the Halo universe, we set out to make durable, stylish gear for anyone fighting the good fight, whether that's the Covenant or a 4am debugging session.
